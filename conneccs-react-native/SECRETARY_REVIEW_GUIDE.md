# Secretary Review Guide - How to Review Submitted IPCRs

**Date:** May 8, 2026  
**For:** Secretary Role Users  
**Purpose:** Complete guide for reviewing and rating faculty-submitted IPCRs

---

## Overview

As a **Secretary**, you are responsible for:
1. **Monitoring faculty compliance** - Track who has submitted their targets
2. **Rating submitted targets** - Provide Q/E/T ratings for faculty accomplishments
3. **Managing incomplete targets** - Return targets that need revision
4. **Forwarding to Dean** - Send rated targets to Dean for final approval

---

## Accessing the Review Queue

### Step 1: Login as Secretary
- Use secretary credentials (e.g., Ichelle Figura-Baluis)
- Navigate to Dashboard

### Step 2: Open Review Queue
Two ways to access:
1. **From Dashboard**: Click "Rating Queue" card or "Start Rating" button
2. **From Quick Actions**: Click "Rating Queue" action card
3. **From Sidebar**: Navigate to "Review Queue"

## How to Rate a Target

### Step 1: Click "Rate This Target"
Opens the rating modal with target details.

### Step 2: Review Target Information
The modal shows:
- Faculty name
- Target description
- Faculty's self-rating average

### Step 3: Enter Your Ratings
Provide ratings for each dimension (1-5 scale):

**Quality (Q): 1-5**
- 5 = Outstanding
- 4 = Very Satisfactory
- 3 = Satisfactory
- 2 = Unsatisfactory
- 1 = Poor

**Efficiency (E): 1-5**
- Same scale as Quality

**Timeliness (T): 1-5**
- Same scale as Quality

**Note:** Only rate the dimensions that are **required** for that target (marked with "x" in OPCR).

### Step 4: See Computed Average
As you enter ratings, the system automatically calculates the average:
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Computed Average:           4.33    │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step 5: Choose Action

**Option A: Rate & Forward to Dean**
- Click "Rate & Forward to Dean" button
- Target is marked as RATED
- Forwarded to Dean's approval queue
- Faculty is notified

**Option B: Mark Incomplete**
- Enter a note explaining what's missing
- Click "Mark Incomplete" button
- Confirm the action
- Target is returned to faculty
- Faculty is notified with your note

## Rating Guidelines

### When to Rate
✅ **Rate the target if:**
- Accomplishment is clearly described
- Evidence/documents are provided
- Target was completed as described
- Self-rating seems reasonable

### When to Mark Incomplete
❌ **Mark incomplete if:**
- Accomplishment description is vague or missing
- No evidence/documents provided
- Target wasn't actually completed
- Self-rating is unrealistic
- More information is needed

### Important Rules

**🚫 You CANNOT rate:**
- Your own IPCR (if you're also faculty)
- The Dean's IPCR (conflict of interest)

The system will show an alert if you try to rate these.

## Compliance Monitoring

### Understanding the Compliance Tab

**Purpose:** Track which faculty have submitted their IPCRs

**Key Metrics:**
- **Total Targets**: How many targets each faculty has
- **Submitted**: How many they've completed (with self-ratings)
- **Pending**: How many are still incomplete
- **Status**: Overall submission status

### Sending Reminders

**When to Send:**
- Faculty has pending targets
- Deadline is approaching
- Faculty hasn't started

**How to Send:**
1. Find faculty in Compliance tab
2. Click 🔔 bell icon in Action column
3. System sends notification to faculty
4. Confirmation: "Reminder sent to [Faculty Name]"

**What Faculty Receives:**
- Notification in their notification panel
- Title: "IPCR Submission Reminder"
- Message: "Please complete and submit your pending IPCR targets"

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: All Targets Rated
**Situation:** Faculty submitted all 63 targets with self-ratings

**What You See:**
- Compliance Tab: "63 submitted, 0 pending"
- Rating Queue Tab: 63 targets awaiting your rating

**What To Do:**
1. Go to Rating Queue tab
2. Rate each target one by one
3. Forward each to Dean
4. Monitor progress

### Scenario 2: Incomplete Evidence
**Situation:** Faculty claims completion but no documents uploaded

**What You See:**
- Target shows "Documents: 0 file(s)"
- Accomplishment text is vague

**What To Do:**
1. Click "Rate This Target"
2. Enter note: "Please upload evidence of completion (documents, photos, certificates, etc.)"
3. Click "Mark Incomplete"
4. Target returns to faculty

### Scenario 3: Late Submission
**Situation:** Faculty submitted after deadline

**What You See:**
- ⚠️ "LATE SUBMISSION" warning on target card
- Target still appears in Rating Queue

**What To Do:**
- You can still rate the target
- Consider timeliness in your T rating
- Late submissions may affect T score
- Follow your institution's late submission policy

### Scenario 4: Unrealistic Self-Rating
**Situation:** Faculty rated themselves 5/5/5 but accomplishment is minimal

**What You See:**
- Faculty Self-Rating: Q: 5, E: 5, T: 5 (Avg: 5.00)
- Accomplishment doesn't match the high rating

**What To Do:**
- Provide your honest rating based on actual accomplishment
- Your rating can differ from self-rating
- Example: You rate Q: 3, E: 3, T: 4 (Avg: 3.33)
- System uses YOUR rating, not self-rating

## Tips for Efficient Review

### 1. Review in Batches
- Set aside dedicated time for rating
- Review similar targets together (e.g., all KRA 1 targets)
- Use consistent rating standards

### 2. Check Documents First
- Always verify evidence is provided
- Download and review uploaded files
- Ensure documents match the accomplishment

### 3. Compare to Target Description
- Read the original target description
- Check if accomplishment matches
- Verify measures were met

### 4. Be Fair and Consistent
- Use the same rating standards for all faculty
- Don't be influenced by personal relationships
- Base ratings on evidence, not assumptions

### 5. Provide Clear Notes
- When marking incomplete, be specific
- Tell faculty exactly what's missing
- Give actionable feedback

## Troubleshooting

### "No targets awaiting rating"
**Cause:** Faculty haven't submitted yet  
**Solution:** Check Compliance tab to see submission status

### "Cannot rate this target"
**Cause:** Trying to rate your own or Dean's IPCR  
**Solution:** Skip these targets, they'll be rated by someone else

### "Invalid rating"
**Cause:** Rating not between 1-5  
**Solution:** Enter valid numbers (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5)

### Target not appearing in queue
**Cause:** Faculty hasn't clicked "Submit IPCR" yet  
**Solution:** Send reminder via Compliance tab

## Need Help?

**Common Questions:**
- **Q: Can I change a rating after submitting?**  
  A: No, once forwarded to Dean, you cannot edit. Be sure before submitting.

- **Q: What if faculty disagrees with my rating?**  
  A: Your rating is based on evidence. Document your reasoning.

- **Q: How long should rating take?**  
  A: 2-5 minutes per target, depending on complexity.

- **Q: Can I rate multiple targets at once?**  
  A: No, rate one target at a time for accuracy.
